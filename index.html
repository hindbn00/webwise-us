Questions people ask

How long does it take?

One-page sites usually take a few days. Five-page sites take about a week or so, depending on how quickly you send content.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, a short description, contact details, and any logo or photos you have. No content yet? Tell us and we'll help.

Do I pay domain or hosting fees?

The package price covers design and building. A domain name (usually about $12 a year) is paid to the registrar. We'll explain the options before you commit.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. You review the draft and we adjust it until you're happy with the result.

How do I pay?

We'll agree on payment details over WhatsApp before work starts.
