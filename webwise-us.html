FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to provide the text and photos?

For Launch and Business, yes: you send your content and we design around it. Growth includes professional copywriting.

How do payments work?

We take 50% upfront to start and 50% when you approve the final site. Card and PayPal accepted.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, the site and its code are yours.

What if I need changes after launch?

Revisions are included during the build. After launch, small updates are available at a fair hourly rate.

Do you build online stores?

Not at the moment. We focus on business websites, landing pages and lead generation.
